Methodology

How the reports are built.

Valuatum AI Equity Reports are built as professional research notes: a company profile, research snapshot, recommendation logic, value breakdown, valuation work, scenarios, catalysts, risks, sources and disclaimer in one structured PDF.

01

Company Profile

Each report begins by defining the business being analysed before any valuation conclusion is shown. This creates a common factual base for the rest of the report.

  • Company description, listing venue, ticker and sector classification
  • Business model, key segments, geographic exposure and revenue drivers
  • Market context and the strategic question the report is trying to answer
  • Relevant financial scale indicators such as revenue, EBIT, market value and enterprise value
02

Research Snapshot

The snapshot summarises the investment case before the detailed sections. It is designed to make the report scannable while keeping the full reasoning available later in the PDF.

  • Recommendation, target price and implied upside/downside where available
  • Thesis breaker: the single issue most likely to invalidate the case
  • Key evidence, main valuation tension and near-term monitoring points
  • Report date, data cut-off and confidence notes where relevant
03

Value Breakdown

The report separates group value into the business units, assets or option pools that actually drive the investment case. The aim is to avoid treating a multi-segment company as a single blended multiple.

  • Segment revenue, EBIT and margin profile where disclosure allows it
  • Enterprise value allocation across the relevant value drivers
  • Interpretation of which segments carry or dilute the group valuation
  • Clear separation between current earnings value and longer-term optionality
04

Recommendation Logic

The recommendation is not treated as a black-box label. The report explains why the rating follows from the valuation evidence, risk profile and scenario range.

  • Current price versus derived target price or fair value range
  • Expected upside/downside and valuation margin of safety
  • Evidence that supports the rating and evidence that could contradict it
  • Short explanation of why the report lands on buy, hold, sell or equivalent language
05

Core Analysis

The core analysis section explains the business rather than only presenting figures. It connects financial development, competitive position and strategic choices to valuation.

  • Business model quality, cyclicality, capital intensity and pricing power
  • Historical performance and key changes in revenue, margin and cash flow
  • Segment-level drivers and the bridge from operations to valuation
  • Financial bridge from actuals to assumptions used in the valuation
06

Valuation & Scenarios

The valuation section ties the operating view to the target price or fair value range. It combines peer multiples, internal assumptions and scenario logic where the available data supports it.

  • Peer multiples, EV-based valuation metrics and target-price derivation
  • Base, bull and bear scenarios with different operational assumptions
  • Sensitivity tables for the most important valuation drivers
  • Reverse-valuation logic where useful: what would need to be true for the current price to be justified
07

Catalysts & Risks

The report does not stop at a static valuation. It identifies what could change the investment case after publication.

  • Positive catalysts that could close a valuation gap or improve the rating
  • Operational, financial and market risks that could weaken the case
  • Early-warning indicators and monitoring checklist items
  • Assessment of which risks matter most for the target price and recommendation
08

Appendix, Sources & Disclaimer

Each report ends with the audit trail needed to review the analysis. The appendix makes the report easier to verify and use as a professional research input.

  • Financial appendix with historical figures, forecast tables and quarterly snapshots where available
  • Source register covering company reports, filings, market data and estimate inputs
  • AI disclosure, assumptions, data limitations and report date
  • Clear disclaimer that the report is informational research material, not investment advice
