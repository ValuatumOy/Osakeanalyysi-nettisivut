Our background

Practitioner-grade analysis at every level.

Valuatum was founded in 2000 by a team of former equity and credit risk analysts. We bring practitioner-grade understanding to every part of our platform — because we have done the work ourselves.

Our flagship products — Valuatum ProFinder for equity research and Valuatum CreditRisk for credit analysis — are used by credit institutions, stockbrokers, investment banks, and asset managers across Europe and beyond.

AI Equity Reports is a new product built on the same analytical foundation — making professional-grade equity research accessible to individual investors, analysts, students, and finance professionals who need a fast, structured starting point.

Why we built this product

→
Analysis takes too long

Starting a company analysis from scratch takes hours. Most investors and analysts don't have that time for every company they look at.

→
Institutional research is gated

Professional equity research is expensive and available only to institutional clients. Individual investors get a fraction of the analysis.

→
Dashboards don't build the case

Most data tools give you more numbers. They don't synthesize them into a structured investment case with a clear analytical logic.

→
AI can be structured properly

When AI is given the right framework — EV discipline, value pool structure, reverse valuation — it can generate analysis that is genuinely useful at a fraction of the traditional cost.

What we will never do

We will never claim to give investment advice

Our reports are research materials. Buy/sell language, price targets, and performance guarantees will not appear in our products.

We will never use misleading valuation framing

No comparing market cap to segment revenue, no inventing "zero value" or "free" business units, no price targets without disclosed assumptions.

We will never hide our limitations

Every report includes a methodology section and known limitations. AI can be wrong. We say so clearly, every time.

We will never use hype to sell reports

No "beat the market" claims, no guaranteed alpha, no "AI knows what stocks will go up." The product sells itself by being genuinely useful.
