Our background

Practitioner-grade analysis at every level.

Valuatum was founded in 2000 by former equity and credit risk analysts. We have spent 25 years building financial analysis tools for professional institutions, so we understand what a useful research tool actually looks like.

Our flagship products — Valuatum ProFinder for equity research and Valuatum CreditRisk for credit analysis — are used by credit institutions, stockbrokers, investment banks, and asset managers across Europe and beyond.

AI Equity Reports runs on the same analytical foundation. It makes professional equity research accessible to individual investors, analysts, students, and finance professionals who need a fast, structured starting point.

Why we built this product

→
Analysis takes too long

Starting a company analysis from scratch takes hours. Most investors and analysts don't have that time for every company they look at.

→
Institutional research is gated

Professional equity research is expensive and available only to institutional clients. Individual investors get a fraction of the analysis.

→
Dashboards don't build the case

Most data tools give you more numbers. They don't synthesize them into a structured investment case with a clear analytical logic.

→
AI can be structured properly

With the right framework (EV discipline, segment value structure, reverse valuation), AI can generate analysis that is genuinely useful at a fraction of traditional cost.

What we will never do

We will never claim to provide personal investment advice

Reports may include analytical rating and target-price language, but they are general research materials — not advice tailored to your portfolio, objectives, or risk tolerance.

We will never pretend valuation is certainty

Valuation outputs depend on assumptions. We focus on enterprise value discipline, value drivers, and reverse valuation so users can inspect what the analysis is assuming.

We will never hide that AI can be wrong

AI-generated analysis can contain errors, omissions, or misweighted assumptions. We explain the methodology and limitations on the site and encourage users to verify key figures.

We will never use hype to sell reports

No guaranteed alpha, no "AI knows what stocks will go up," and no certainty theatre. The product should earn trust by being useful and clear about its assumptions.
