Executive summary

Orion Corporation (ORNBV.HE, NASDAQ Helsinki) is a Finnish pharmaceutical company whose growth profile is increasingly shaped by the rapid scale-up of Nubeqa royalties from its partnership with Bayer; in this Valuatum equity research report dated 7 July 2026 we rate the stock BUY with a 12-month target price of 74.80 EUR against a current price of 70.50 EUR, implying roughly 6.1% upside. Market capitalisation is EUR 9.9 bn and enterprise value is EUR 10 bn.

The core valuation tension is extreme concentration: approximately 83.6% of the EUR 10,030 million group enterprise value (EUR 8,385 million) is allocated to the Innovative Medicines segment and its Nubeqa royalty stream, even though the segment generates only about 34% of current group revenue. Adjusting reported 2024 segment EBIT of EUR 289 million for EUR 130 million of positive one-off milestones and EUR 41 million of pipeline write-downs leaves underlying recurring operating profit closer to EUR 200 million, so the allocation implies a demanding multiple of over 40x trailing recurring EBIT, while the legacy divisions supplying nearly two-thirds of revenue carry minimal valuation weight. On a reverse-valuation basis the embedded assumptions are already priced but achievable: Bayer's Nubeqa in-market sales of EUR 1.52 bn in 2024 must clear EUR 3.0 bn by 2027-2030 to justify the Innovative Medicines allocation, lifting group EBIT from EUR 417 million in 2024 to EUR 694 million by 2027. The base scenario implies EUR 70.46 per share (-0.1%), essentially the current price; the Bull scenario with label expansions lifts the value to EUR 84.45 (+19.8%), while a Nubeqa plateau near EUR 2 billion in the Bear case would compress the shares to EUR 45.35 (-35.7%).
