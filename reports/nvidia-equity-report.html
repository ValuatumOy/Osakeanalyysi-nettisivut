The market values the segment because it captures a large share of the global AI infrastructure buildout — a highly profitable, immediate cash-generating business rather than a speculative option — with investors assigning a mid-30x trailing EBIT multiple because they expect the shift from AI training workloads to AI inference workloads to keep expanding the total addressable market. The moat rests mainly on system-level integration rather than pure silicon performance: the CUDA software ecosystem provides a strong developer-level moat, while the physical moat in 2026 relies on proprietary networking — NVLink and InfiniBand — with systems like the GB300 NVL72 functioning essentially as a single, large logical GPU.
