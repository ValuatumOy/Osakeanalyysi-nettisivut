Executive summary

Kesko Oyj (KESKOB.HE, NASDAQ Helsinki) is a Finnish trading group with three divisions — grocery trade, building and technical trade, and car trade; in this Valuatum equity research report dated 7 July 2026 we rate the stock SELL with a 12-month target price of 17.10 EUR against a current price of 19.20 EUR, implying roughly 10.9% downside. Market capitalisation is EUR 7.6 bn and enterprise value is EUR 11.1 bn.

The core valuation tension is a flat blended multiple applied to very different businesses.
