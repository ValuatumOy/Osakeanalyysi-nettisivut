Executive summary

Stora Enso Oyj (STERV.HE, NASDAQ Helsinki) operates renewable-materials businesses spanning consumer paperboard, containerboard, corrugated packaging, market pulp, sawn and engineered wood, and forest assets in the Nordics and Latin America. The recommendation is BUY with a 12-month target price of 15.80 EUR against a current price of 9.44 EUR, implying +67.4% upside. Market capitalisation is EUR 7.4 bn and enterprise value is EUR 10.9 bn in this Valuatum equity research report dated 18 August 2026. The investment case combines forest-asset recognition with recovery in Oulu-led consumer packaging earnings.

The central tension is that Forest and Land Assets receive 45.4% of current EV, or EUR 4,941m, despite contributing only 5.1% of revenue and 19.9% of comparable EBIT. Consumer Packaging and Biomaterials contribute 34.7% of EV, while industrial earnings remain cyclical and capital intensive. The stock trades at 20.0x 2026E P/E and 7.6x 2026E EV/EBITDA, but depressed 2026 EBIT makes the current EV/EBIT multiple 15.8x. The valuation rests less on a normal industrial recovery than on a demerger making forest value observable and Oulu converting capacity into profitable sales.

The target price is a 100%-weighted sum-of-the-parts valuation: Consumer Packaging at 6.0x 2027E EBITDA, Integrated Packaging at 6.0x, Biomaterials at 6.3x, Wood Products and Building Solutions at 8.0x, and Forest and Land Assets at 12.0x modeled EBITDA plus a discounted appraisal cross-check. The scenario bridge gives Bear at 7.52 EUR per share and -20.3%, Base at 15.75 EUR and +66.9%, and Bull at 23.60 EUR and +150.0%. The 15.80 EUR target sits between Base and Bull.

Investment thesis — three reasons

01 Forest value surfaces EUR 7.2bn

The base SOTP values Forest and Land Assets at EUR 7.2bn, below the EUR 8.5bn reported forest-asset fair value. More than 1.2m hectares are planned for demerger into Bergslagets skogar in H1 2027, creating a quoted reference for an asset currently embedded in a low-return industrial group.

02 Oulu earnings inflect EUR 800m

The Oulu BM6 line has 750,000 tonnes of annual capacity and targets approximately EUR 800m of full-capacity sales by 2027. EBITDA reached breakeven by end-2025, but Q4 ramp costs reduced adjusted EBIT by EUR 31m. The valuation requires saleable utilization to rise towards 85–90%.

03 Market prices failure EUR 592m

The reverse valuation requires only EUR 286m of 2027 EBIT, versus the maintained forecast of EUR 878m, a EUR 592m gap. This implies a prolonged earnings relapse rather than a normal cyclical trough. Even partial Oulu delivery and forest recognition could therefore support re-rating without full peer-level returns.

Thesis breaker: Oulu underperformance combined with a materially impaired or delayed forest demerger would invalidate the base SOTP and remove both principal routes to value realization.

Scenarios and verdict

The Bear case requires Oulu to remain below roughly 65% saleable utilization, weak European containerboard utilization, lower pulp pricing and a delayed forest separation with larger deductions. The Base case requires 85–90% Oulu utilization, normalized packaging spreads, better Biomaterials fluff mix and an H1 2027 demerger. The Bull case requires full Oulu capacity, approximately EUR 800m of sales, successful packaging price initiatives, higher wood-product returns and stronger cash generation that reduces debt. The resulting share prices are 7.52 EUR (-20.3%) in Bear, 15.75 EUR (+66.9%) in Base and 23.60 EUR (+150.0%) in Bull. Early indicators are Oulu saleable tonnes and repeat orders, the forest prospectus deductions, containerboard net prices after announced increases, operating rates near 82%, pulp price relative to delivered fibre cost and wood-product EBIT per cubic metre. The central debate is whether forest value will become distributable public value before industrial cash leakage consumes it, or whether Oulu and cyclical spreads must first repair returns. The scheduled demerger and Oulu ramp make the balance of evidence supportive of BUY, but failure on both would invalidate the target.
