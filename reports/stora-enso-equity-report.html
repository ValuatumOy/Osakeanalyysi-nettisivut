About Stora Enso

Stora Enso Oyj is a Finland-headquartered renewable materials group producing fiber-based packaging materials and solutions, biomaterials, and wood products, while also owning substantial forest assets in the Nordics and Baltics. The company is restructuring its reporting and preparing to separate its Swedish forest assets into a new independently listed forest company.

Executive summary

Stora Enso Oyj (STERV.HE, NASDAQ Helsinki) is a renewable materials, packaging and forest assets group. This Valuatum equity research report dated 24 July 2026 rates the stock BUY with a 12-month target price of EUR 13.30 versus a current price of EUR 9.17, implying roughly +45.0% upside.
