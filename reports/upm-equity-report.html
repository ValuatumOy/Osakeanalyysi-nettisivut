Scenarios and verdict

The Bear case requires a lasting failure of global hardwood pulp prices to recover, leaving Fibres at an 8% margin, while Leuna commissioning delays cause half of the EUR 1,275m sunk capex to be written off. The resulting share price is 15.93 EUR, or -31.9%. The Base case requires normalized hardwood pulp prices, a fully ramped Paso de los Toros mill, stable Nordic power prices and Leuna remaining on track; it produces 23.57 EUR per share, or +0.7%. The Bull case requires hardwood pulp prices above USD 750/t, issue-free Leuna production, validation of the BioMEG green premium and growth valuation for the target EBIT, producing 30.46 EUR per share, or +30.2%. Early evidence will come from the South American eucalyptus pulp spot price, Paso de los Toros operating continuity, Leuna commissioning updates and Olkiluoto 3 outage performance. The central debate is whether UPM Fibres is at a cyclical trough that can normalize, or permanently exposed to new Latin American supply and weak Chinese demand, while Leuna either validates or destroys its option value. The balance of evidence supports BUY because the physical assets are in place, Energy is earning its allocation and Fibres needs normalization rather than sustained price inflation.
