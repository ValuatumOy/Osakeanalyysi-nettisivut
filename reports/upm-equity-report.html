What the share price is made of therefore depends on delivery across several distinct businesses, not one uniform earnings path.

Pulp and timber needs a 13% margin against EUR 283m of 2025 comparable EBIT; adhesive materials needs 11% against 7.5%; specialty papers needs 16% against 11.2%; and graphic paper needs 11% after a Q3 2026 comparable EBIT loss of EUR 8m. Forestry is valued on EUR 2bn of assets, not profit. The peer multiples do not remove the execution risk embedded in those forecast margins.

The European Commission had expressed serious concerns, so the potential EUR 50m annual UPM saving remains conditional. Biochemicals has the longest execution chain: integrated production is expected in Q2 2027, nominal capacity in early 2028, and disclosed annual volume must eventually support 568,538 tonnes by 2035. These indicators determine whether the existing pools earn their forecast profits and whether the residual is supported by a real business.
