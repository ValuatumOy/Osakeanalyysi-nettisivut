Reverse valuation

The scenario bridge tests how the valuation changes with the duration of the hyperscaler capex cycle and oligopoly pricing discipline. The primary swing factor is whether new Kioxia, Samsung and other capacity reaches the market before AI inference demand absorbs it. A traditional NAND glut and 30% ASP decline would largely eliminate operating profit, while sustained tight capacity permits elevated pricing across Cloud, Client and Consumer.
