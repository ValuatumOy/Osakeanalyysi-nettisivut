Executive summary

Wärtsilä Oyj Abp (WRT1V.HE, NASDAQ Helsinki) makes ship engines, propulsion equipment, maintenance services and engine-based power plants, while its Valo joint venture participates in grid-scale battery storage. The shares traded at 25.8 EUR on 11 November 2026, giving a market capitalisation of EUR 15.2bn and an enterprise value of EUR 12.8bn. In this Valuatum market report dated 11 November 2026, analysts forecast operating profit of EUR 1.1bn for 2028 on sales of EUR 7.5bn. The price therefore reflects a group with a large marine franchise, a substantial energy business and a smaller storage operation whose profitability remains the least established.

The share price is made up chiefly of Marine at 14.3 EUR per share, or 14.1x its 2028 forecast operating profit of EUR 597m. Energy contributes 5.4 EUR per share at 7.9x forecast operating profit of EUR 405m, while Energy Storage contributes 2.0 EUR per share at 9.2x forecast operating profit of EUR 125m. The three businesses total 21.6 EUR per share. Cash, less debt and other claims, adds 4.1 EUR per share, bringing the split to 25.8 EUR. Marine accounts for 53% of 2028 operating profit in the split, Energy 36% and Energy Storage 11%; these are EBIT shares, not shares of enterprise value. The businesses together cost about 11.5x 2028 operating profit, against about 15x for listed peers. Marine is priced below its peer median of 17.7x, Energy below its 12.8x median and Energy Storage at the industry's lower quartile of 9.2x versus an 11.4x median. The reading will change with Marine's adjusted margin moving from 12.7% toward 14%, Energy moving from 15.4% toward 18%, and Valo turning positive toward the end of 2027. The 12-vessel CMA CGM engine order, separate 2027 business guidance, data-centre deliveries, order intake and storage margins are the practical tests.
