About Self Storage Group

Self Storage Group ASA operates as a Scandinavian self-storage provider, offering rentable storage space through its City Self-Storage brand and related facilities in Norway. The company focuses on leasing units to private and business customers, supported by lean operations, automation, and customer service. It has also indicated a longer-term brand transition toward Green Storage.
