Oriola Stock Analysis: Is ORIOLA.HE Undervalued?

Lauri Hynönen · 27 July 2026 · 7 min read

Is Oriola stock undervalued in 2026?

Yes, on our numbers. Our 1 June 2026 equity report rates Oriola Oyj (ORIOLA.HE) a BUY with a 1.15 EUR twelve-month target against a 0.93 EUR price, about 23.5% upside. The stock trades near 0.90 EUR today (27 July 2026), so the gap has widened a little since the report date.

The screen tells a different story. Oriola carries a negative 2026E P/E of -9.6x, and its reported net sales just fell from roughly EUR 1.9 billion to about EUR 210 million. Neither of those is what it looks like. One is an accounting change, the other is a joint venture sitting below the operating line.

Look at the operating business instead. Oriola's EUR 173 million equity value (EUR 281 million enterprise value) sits on top of a core distribution operation that produces 100% of the group's EUR 44 million forward EBITDA. Our view is that the market is discounting the wrong thing.

Why does Oriola's P/E look negative and net sales collapse?

Two accounting facts, not two disasters. In 2026 Oriola changed its revenue recognition to an agent model under IFRS 15, recognizing only the distribution fee rather than the gross value of the drugs it moves. The company states the change leaves EBITDA and cash generation unaffected (Oriola stock exchange release, 2026). In our model that restatement collapses reported net sales from about EUR 1.9 billion to roughly EUR 210 million and lifts the reported EBITDA margin from near 1% to over 20%.

The negative P/E is the second artifact. Because Kronans Apotek is a 50% joint venture, its net losses flow into Oriola's pre-tax profit but its revenue and EBITDA sit below the operating line. Strong group EBITDA and negative net earnings coexist. So the headline P/E is close to useless here. EV/EBITDA at 6.4x (2026E) is the fairer lens.

What is Oriola actually worth? A sum-of-the-parts view

Our EUR 281 million enterprise value splits into three pools in the 1 June 2026 model. The distribution business does the work; the joint venture holds the argument.

Services is the profit engine: a regulated Nordic distribution duopoly, main competitor Tamro, valued at an undemanding ~4.7x. Products is a cleaner wholesale operation after the April 2025 sale of Svensk dos AB, also near 4.8x. Together those two core pools carry the full EUR 109 million forward net debt without heroic assumptions. That leaves the JV as the swing factor. Among Helsinki-listed stocks, Oriola sits in the same defensive bucket as grocery and pharmacy names such as Kesko, but with a turnaround option bolted on.

The whole thesis is Kronans Apotek

One asset drives almost all of the upside. The market assigns the 50% Kronans Apotek stake only about EUR 75 million of EV against a EUR 187 million book value. That is a roughly 60% discount (0.4x price-to-book), a gap of about EUR 112 million.

The discount has a cause. A flawed 2024 to 2025 ERP integration disrupted store operations, cost market share, and forced two identical impairments of about EUR 16 million each, in Q4 2024 (Oriola release, 2024) and again in Q4 2025 (Oriola release, 2025). Kronans is Sweden's third-largest pharmacy chain, with a 20.7% market share in 2025.

Here is the assumption doing the heavy lifting: we assume the JV recovers toward an industry-standard 3 to 4% retail EBIT margin by 2027, at which point its book value can be realized through resumed dividends or a sale. Inderes has floated a disposal of the stake as a logical first step (Oninvest). The named risk to our view: if the losses instead drain liquidity, the two 50% owners face a dilutive capital call. That is the thesis-breaker.

What Oriola is worth if the JV turns, and if it doesn't

The scenario spread is wide because it all hinges on one variable. The table below brackets the two outcomes for the joint venture against today's numbers. Our 1.15 EUR twelve-month target sits between them, a deliberately conservative near-term call.

The upside case is not our base target. It needs the turnaround to happen, and Swedish retail pharmacy margins have been the problem, not the solution, for two years. If the turnaround fails, the core distribution business and its EUR 44 million EBITDA still exist, but a capital call would push equity value down toward EUR 40 million against a EUR 173 million market cap today. That is the trade in front of you: a binary on one Swedish pharmacy chain, priced as if the turnaround has already failed.

Frequently asked questions

Is Oriola a buy in 2026?

On our 1 June 2026 model, yes. We rate Oriola Oyj (ORIOLA.HE) BUY with a 1.15 EUR twelve-month target against a 0.93 EUR price, about 23.5% upside. The call rests on a stable distribution core that supports the debt while the discounted Kronans Apotek joint venture re-rates.

Why does Oriola have a negative P/E?

Because its 50% Kronans Apotek joint venture is loss-making at the net level, and those losses flow into Oriola's pre-tax profit while the JV's revenue and EBITDA sit below the operating line. Group EBITDA stays positive at about EUR 44 million forward, so we treat EV/EBITDA of 6.4x, not the -9.6x P/E, as the fair lens.

What is Oriola's price target?

Our 1 June 2026 report sets a 1.15 EUR twelve-month target, about 23.5% above the 0.93 EUR price at the report date. If Kronans Apotek re-rates toward its EUR 187 million book value by 2027, our upside case values the equity near EUR 1.54 per share; a failed turnaround points much lower.

Why is Oriola undervalued?

Two accounting effects hide the operating business. A 2026 IFRS 15 agent restatement cut reported net sales from about EUR 1.9 billion to EUR 210 million, and the loss-making JV drags net earnings negative. Underneath, the core distribution duopoly generates EUR 44 million forward EBITDA at an undemanding 4.7x EV/EBITDA.

What is the biggest risk to Oriola stock?

A Kronans Apotek capital call. If the joint venture's losses exhaust its liquidity buffers, the two 50% owners could be forced into a dilutive capital injection, the report's thesis-breaker. In that downside the core carries the full net debt and our scenario math points to equity value near EUR 40 million.
