What an instruction can change.

Can change

  • A business’s multiple, sales or margin
  • Model variables by year: tax rate, beta, payout, working capital, investment
  • A market’s size, and a new business’s outcomes and chances
  • How the company is divided into businesses
  • What the text explains and what it keeps short

Stays fixed

  • Reported financial figures
  • The target itself, which follows from the assumptions
  • Businesses the analyst leaves alone, which keep the market’s view
  • Facts without a source: a missing fact is looked up and cited
