How a report is made.

From the market price to an analyst’s own report in five steps, shown with a real one: Remedy Entertainment, 28 October 2026.

  1. 1Market

    The company and its share price

    The starting point is what investors pay today, not a value the AI invents.

    Remedy Entertainment · €17.40 a share · market cap €236m
  2. 2Valuatum model

    Statements and forecasts

    Reported financials and analysts’ consensus forecasts in Valuatum’s financial model, which calculates every forecast the report values.

    2025 sales €59.5m · 2028 consensus: sales €123m, operating profit €16m
  3. 3AI

    The base report

    AI researches the businesses, peers and news, and splits the price between the businesses: what each must deliver for the price to hold.

    Games carries the whole price: 17.6× its 2028 profit, the peer median. Main test: CONTROL Resonant, “scheduled for 24 September”.
  4. 4Analyst

    The analyst’s instruction

    In plain language: where the analyst disagrees, or what the model could not know. A multiple, a margin, the size of a business, a fact.

    The game launched on 24 September and sold over a million copies by 15 October. Update the text; change no figures.
  5. 5Back to you

    The updated report

    Rebuilt on the analyst’s assumptions through the same model, with a 12-month target that follows from them and a record of what changed.

    Launch and sales described · forecasts unchanged · 12-month target €17.4, equal to the price

Not there yet? Write another instruction. Each round starts from the latest version, so earlier changes carry forward.
