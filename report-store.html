Every report on one page.

For each company: the engine's own report, and every analyst's published analysis on top of it. The analyses are ordered by what other analysts said they added — a score out of five and a written comparison, from people who had to read the report to give it.

An analyst analysis is the engine's report re-run with that analyst's own assumptions and instructions, published under their own name. The rating measures what the analyst's work added over the base report — not how good the base report was. Reading one costs a monthly allowance and a written review, which is why the ratings come from people who actually read it.
