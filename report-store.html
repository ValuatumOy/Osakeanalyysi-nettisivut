Every report on one page.

For each company: the engine's own report, and every analyst report published on top of it. The analyst reports are ordered by what other analysts said they added, a score out of five and a written comparison, from people who had to read the report to give it. To buy a Valuatum report on its own, or order a fresh one for a company nobody has covered yet, use the reports page.

An analyst report is the engine's report re-run with that analyst's own assumptions and instructions, published under their own name. The rating measures what the analyst's work added over the engine's report, not how good that report was. Reading one costs a member a monthly allowance and a written review, which is why the ratings come from people who actually read it. Anyone can read the engine's free weekly reports, and the analyst reports we hand-pick into a free window. Those say Free to read.
