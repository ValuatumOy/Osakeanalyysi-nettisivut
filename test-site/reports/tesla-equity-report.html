  • Robotaxi economics: the USD 0.30 per mile operating-cost goal was unmet in October 2026, while the price requires USD 1.02tn of 2040 sales; cost per mile and paid rides are the early warnings.
  • Robotaxi funding: Tesla expects 2026 capital spending above USD 20bn and has USD 30bn of credit lines; capital spending and fleet additions are the early warnings.

  • Cars margin: the 2028 split assumes a 6% margin while the company-wide third-quarter margin was 2.6% and a USD 475m credit reversal reduced revenue; automotive cost of revenue and deliveries are the early warnings.
  • Energy normalisation: third-quarter gross margin benefited from tariff refunds and reimbursed cell costs; gross margin excluding these items and storage deployments are the early warnings.

What to watch

  • Optimus capacity and unit economics (medium-term, Optimus): stated capacity, output approaching the one-million-unit first-line plan and costs near the 20,000-30,000 USD selling goal would test scale.
