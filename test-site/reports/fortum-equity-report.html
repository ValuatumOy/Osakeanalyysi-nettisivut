Executive summary

Fortum Oyj (FORTUM.HE, NASDAQ Helsinki) generates electricity from Nordic hydro, nuclear and operating wind assets and sells electricity and related services to consumers through Generation, Consumer Solutions and Other Operations. The HOLD rating carries a 12-month target price of 22.30 EUR against a current price of 20.76 EUR, implying +7.4% upside, with market capitalisation of EUR 18.6 bn and enterprise value of EUR 20.8 bn, in this Valuatum equity research report dated 30 September 2026. The company also studies potential new nuclear capacity in Finland and Sweden, but that option is not in the 2027–2034 group earnings forecast.

The valuation tension is between a large established-generation earnings base and demanding market allocation. Existing hydro carries 53.2% of EV, or EUR 11,083m, against EUR 580m of modelled 2027 EBIT; existing nuclear carries 31.2%, or EUR 6,495m, against EUR 312m of modelled EBIT; retail carries 15.6%, or EUR 3,248m, against EUR 122m. The price therefore requires hydro capture, nuclear availability and retail margin recovery rather than newbuild earnings.

The target price is built with an EV/EBIT parts-sum: 23.3x 2027E EBIT for hydro and existing nuclear, 14.7x for wind and solar, 16.1x for retail, and 22.2x for EUR 89m of corporate and unallocated EBIT costs.
