Scenarios and verdict

The bear case requires continued double-digit net-price erosion, contracting employer access, rapid normalisation of oral Wegovy's early share and zero value for both option pools; it produces -157.59 DKK per share and -131.6%. The base case requires public access to offset selective employer pullbacks, oral and higher-dose formulations to expand treated volume, Ozempic to decline gradually rather than collapse, and probability-weighted options to retain value; it produces 744.86 DKK and +49.5%. The bull case requires stronger patient conversion and persistence, stable blended net price, successful CagriSema and zenagamtide evidence, and operating leverage; it produces 2,839.49 DKK and +470.1%. Early indicators are Medicare starts and persistence, weekly oral share paired with net price, 2027 revenue after the US price reset, REDEFINE 11, Awiqli switching and Alhemo and Sogroya patient starts. The central investment debate is whether Novo can retain enough reimbursed volume and persistence to offset net-price erosion, or whether Lilly-led competition and patent-driven replacement outrun its formulation and pipeline response. Oral prescriptions and CKD or MASH permissions support the first view, while 22% injectable price erosion, Lilly's 60.1% combined US position and CagriSema's 23.0% versus 25.5% efficacy support caution. The balance supports BUY, but only with close monitoring of realised price and share.
