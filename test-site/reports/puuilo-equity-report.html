The strategy combines Finnish like-for-like growth with expansion towards more than 100 stores by FY2030 and an initial Swedish rollout.

The bear assumes saturation and margin reversal; the bull requires high-single-digit like-for-like growth, at least eight productive annual openings and stable inventory efficiency.
