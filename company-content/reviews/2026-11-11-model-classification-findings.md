# EBIT classification checks — 11 November 2026

These comparisons use the production Wisdom annual income statement for FY 2025 and the issuers' consolidated annual financial statements. Amounts are in EUR millions. The overview pages show the issuer-reported EBIT; the findings below do not establish why the source model classifies the items differently.

## Aallon Group

| Item | Wisdom | Annual report |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| EBIT | 3.009 | 2.970 |
| Financial income | 0.082 | 0.082 |
| Interest/financial expenses | -0.293 | -0.293 |
| Other financial expenses | -0.039 | Not separately presented below EBIT |
| Profit before tax | 2.759 | 2.759 |

The 0.039 difference in EBIT equals the model's separately classified other financial expenses, and pretax profit agrees. This is consistent with a classification difference, but the underlying source item and the reason for the classification have not been established. Wisdom's EBITDA and depreciation also differ from the issuer's reported components, so these figures should not be used to infer a specific source transaction without further investigation.

Source: [Aallon Group annual report 2025](https://aallon.fi/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/20260223_AALLON-Vuosikatsaus-2025-.pdf); production Wisdom income statement, `AALLON.HE`, FY 2025, read 2026-11-11.
